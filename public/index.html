Working on it

    How it works

    Bookers match acts to a room by style and size. Booker measures both with Qloo: style is what the fans of your best shows also love (Qloo affinity); size is Qloo's popularity of the acts your room already drew. Every number on the result traces to a Qloo call, listed under "How we know".

    Booker uses Qloo for taste data and Open-Meteo for city lookup. Qloo describes what groups of fans tend to like; it doesn't know ticket prices, fees, routing or availability. For AI agents: Booker's MCP server is at (tools find_acts_for_venue and find_rooms_for_artist).
